Privacy Policy

CloneE is designed to work without accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, or a developer-operated backend.

Effective date: September 17, 2026 · Last updated: September 17, 2026

Privacy at a glance

  • No advertising.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No analytics or behavioral profiling.
  • No CloneE account is required.
  • No personal data is sent to a CloneE-operated server.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy applies to the CloneE iOS application (“CloneE”, the “App”) and this website. CloneE is provided by the developer identified on the App Store listing (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

2. Information CloneE collects

We do not collect personal information through the App. CloneE does not require registration and does not operate a developer-controlled server that receives gameplay or profile data.

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, device advertising identifiers, browsing history, search history, health data, financial information, or other personal information through CloneE.

3. Information stored locally on your device

To make the game work and remember your preferences, CloneE may store limited information locally on your device, such as:

  • BEST score and basic gameplay statistics;
  • selected difficulty;
  • mute preference;
  • selected emoji;
  • custom emoji added by a Premium user; and
  • other small settings needed to preserve the App's state.

This information is not uploaded to a CloneE-operated server. Local app data may be included in device backups according to your iOS and iCloud settings, which are controlled by Apple and by you.

4. Premium purchase and Restore Purchase

CloneE may offer an optional one-time Premium in-app purchase. Purchases and restoration are processed by Apple using StoreKit and the App Store.

We do not receive or store your payment card number or banking details. Apple may process purchase-related information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. CloneE may ask Apple's StoreKit system whether the App Store account currently has the Premium entitlement so that purchased features can be unlocked or restored.

5. Sharing

CloneE may let you intentionally share game content using Apple's system share interface. Sharing begins only when you choose the Share feature and then select a destination yourself.

The receiving app or service you choose may process the information you share according to its own privacy policy. CloneE does not control those third-party services.

6. Ads, analytics, tracking, and profiling

CloneE does not contain advertising, third-party advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or tracking technology used to follow you across apps or websites. We do not sell personal information and do not create advertising profiles.

7. Third-party services

The App relies on standard Apple platform services where necessary, including iOS, the App Store, StoreKit, system sharing, and related Apple services. Apple may process information required to provide those services under its own privacy practices.

We do not currently integrate a developer-selected third-party advertising or analytics provider into CloneE.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not receive or store your personal information on a CloneE-operated server, we do not maintain a server-side user record for you to delete.

Local CloneE data remains on your device until you reset it through an available in-app control, delete the App, or otherwise remove the App's local data through iOS. The in-app Reset BEST control resets the BEST score. Deleting CloneE removes the App's local container from the device, subject to any backups managed through your Apple settings.

App Store purchase records are maintained by Apple, not by CloneE, and are subject to Apple's retention and account-management practices.

9. Your choices

You can choose whether to purchase Premium, whether to add custom emoji, whether to use the Share feature, whether to mute the App, and whether to reset your BEST score. If you do not want CloneE to retain local game data, you can delete the App from your device.

10. Children's privacy

CloneE does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults through the App. Because the App does not operate accounts, advertising, analytics, or a developer-controlled collection service, we do not build profiles about children or other users.

11. Security

CloneE minimizes privacy risk by keeping its game data local and by not operating a server that receives personal gameplay data. Device-level storage and backup protections are provided by iOS and, where enabled by you, Apple services.

12. International data transfers

We do not transfer personal information to a CloneE-operated server in another country because we do not collect that information through the App. Apple services used for App Store purchases, restoration, device backups, or system functionality may process information internationally under Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

13. Changes to this policy

If CloneE's data practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the “Last updated” date above. If a future version adds a service that collects personal information, the policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before or alongside that change.
