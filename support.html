Premium & purchases

I bought Premium but it is not unlocked.

Open CloneE → Settings → Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed into the same App Store account used for the original purchase.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. CloneE Premium is intended as a one-time lifetime purchase. The App Store purchase sheet shows the current product and price before you confirm payment.

Does reinstalling CloneE remove Premium?

The local app data may be removed when the App is deleted, but an eligible non-consumable Premium purchase can be restored through Apple's StoreKit system using the App Store account that purchased it.

Custom emoji

How do I add an emoji?

Premium users can use the + control in the emoji selector to add a custom emoji. CloneE accepts a single emoji character, including supported composed emoji such as flags, skin-tone variants, and family emoji.

Where are custom emoji stored?

They are stored locally on your device. CloneE does not upload your custom emoji to a developer-operated server.

Gameplay & settings

Can I change difficulty?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Easy, Normal, or Hard. Normal is the standard CloneE difficulty.

How do I mute CloneE?

Open Settings and turn on Mute App.

How do I reset BEST?

Open Settings and choose Reset BEST. CloneE asks for confirmation before resetting the score.
