Gameplay

How do I play?

Pick an emoji, tap the live target before time runs out, and avoid every emoji clone already left on the board. Missing the live target or touching an old clone ends the run.

What is Shuffle?

Shuffle is a Premium gameplay mode where the live target changes emoji as you play instead of using the same selected emoji for the whole run. It uses CloneE’s bundled emoji catalog, so no emoji data needs to be downloaded from a CloneE server.

What is Rush?

Rush is a Premium 30-second mode. Tap open space to place as many emojis as you can before time runs out. Tapping an existing emoji wastes the tap, but it does not end the run.

What is Impostor?

Impostor is a Premium 60-second mode. Each round shows a group of repeated emojis with one different emoji. Tap the odd one out to score and move straight to the next round. A wrong tap ends the run.

What happens when I reach 100?

Nothing interrupts your run. CloneE silently records the difficulty you reached 100 on, clears the accumulated emoji scars so there is fresh space, and lets the same run continue. Your Home screen then shows your 💯 CLUB progress.

Can I earn 💯 CLUB on every difficulty?

Yes. Easy, Normal, and Hard are tracked separately. Reaching 100 on one difficulty does not automatically unlock the others.

Can I change difficulty?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Easy, Normal, or Hard. Difficulty applies to Normal and Shuffle only.

What does Reset All Bests do?

Reset All Bests permanently clears the BEST scores for Normal, Shuffle, Rush, and Impostor, along with your 💯 CLUB achievement state. CloneE asks for confirmation before doing this.

How do I mute CloneE?

Open Settings and enable Mute App.

Premium & purchases

What does Premium unlock?

Premium permanently unlocks Shuffle, Rush, Impostor, and custom emoji management. It is one lifetime unlock for all of those features.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. CloneE Premium is a one-time non-consumable purchase. The App Store purchase sheet shows the current localized price before you confirm payment.

I bought Premium but it is not unlocked.

Open CloneE → Settings → Restore Purchase. Make sure the device is signed into the same App Store account used for the original purchase.

Does reinstalling CloneE remove Premium?

Deleting the App can remove local game data, but an eligible non-consumable Premium purchase can be restored through Apple’s StoreKit system using the App Store account that purchased it.

Custom emoji

How do I add an emoji?

Premium users can tap the purple + control in the Home emoji selector to add a supported custom emoji.

Where are my custom emoji stored?

They are stored locally on your device. CloneE does not upload them to a developer-operated server.
