Choose your challenge

Six modes. Six ways to fill the screen.

Normal is always free. The other five modes come with one-time Premium. Bounce and Key Hunt arrive with version 1.0.3.

❤️Free

Normal

Tap the live emoji before time runs out. Every successful tap leaves a clone. Touch an old one or miss, and it’s over.

🔀Premium

Shuffle

Same reflex challenge as Normal, but every new target is a different randomly selected emoji.

⚡Premium

Rush

Thirty seconds to fill the screen. Tap open space as fast as you can and build up a wall of emojis.

👀Premium

Impostor

Find the one emoji that doesn’t belong. Fast correct answers can give you a little extra time.

🔥Premium · 1.0.3

Bounce

Catch the moving emoji. Every catch leaves a stationary clone behind. There’s no target timer — just avoid the traps.

🗝️Premium · 1.0.3

Key Hunt

Track the old key 🗝️ through moving gold keys 🔑. Every catch creates another decoy.

Easy, Normal and Hard difficulty are available for Normal, Shuffle, Bounce and Key Hunt. Rush and Impostor have their own rules.

The milestone

Earn your place in 💯 CLUB.

Reach 100 in Normal without breaking your flow. The board clears and your run continues; Easy, Normal and Hard each have their own milestone.
